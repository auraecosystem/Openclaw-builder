# Qullamaggie Parabolic Short

Mean-reversion short setup. A stock goes vertical, becomes crowded and unsustainably extended, then snaps back toward its moving averages.

## Definition Of Parabolic

- Large-cap names priced above $50: up at least 50% in the last 10 trading days
- Small-cap names priced at or below $50: up at least 300% in the last 10 trading days
- Usually 3-5 consecutive green days into the setup
- General liquidity and universe filters still apply

## Exact Setup Definition

This is a mean-reversion short, not an anticipatory fade. The edge comes from waiting until the move is stretched and the first clear failure appears.

## Scanning For Candidates

Build the watchlist from names showing true exhaustion characteristics:

- If price is above $50, up at least 50% in the last 10 trading days
- If price is at or below $50, up at least 300% in the last 10 trading days
- Three to five or more consecutive green days into the setup
- Clear separation from the 10-day / 20-day moving averages
- Preferably a crowded, obvious, emotionally driven move

Exclude immediately:

- Day 1 and day 2 moves with no failure yet
- Orderly institutional uptrends that are strong but not parabolic
- Names with impossible borrow or untradeable spreads
- Situations where the only reason to short is “it has gone up a lot”

## Entry Methods

1. Short the opening range low on the first red day using 1-minute or 5-minute candles.
2. Wait for the first red 5-minute candle if momentum keeps pushing at the open.
3. Wait for a decline, a bounce back into VWAP, and a red rejection candle at VWAP, then short the failure.

**Timing**

- He describes day 3 or day 4 after the surge as the highest-probability timing
- Do not short day 1 or day 2 just because a stock looks extended

> "Don't be too early. WAIT for the right setup."

## What A Good Setup Looks Like

- The move is truly climactic for the stock's price bucket
- The stock is on day 3 or day 4 of the mania
- Intraday failure finally appears through OR low failure, first red day, or VWAP rejection
- The setup gives a clear stop level at the day high or VWAP reclaim
- Crowd behavior looks emotional rather than orderly

## What A Weak Setup Looks Like

- The stock is only mildly extended
- The trend is still clean and orderly with no clear failure
- No first red day or VWAP failure has appeared
- The move is large in dollars but not actually parabolic in percentage terms
- Liquidity or borrow quality makes execution unreliable

## Trap Setups And Red Herrings

- **Early short trap:** shorting on day 1 or day 2 only because the chart feels “too high”
- **Strong-trend trap:** mistaking a powerful leader for a finished parabola before failure appears
- **No-trigger trap:** seeing exhaustion emotionally but entering before OR low or VWAP failure
- **Squeeze-back-through-VWAP trap:** entering the VWAP-failure short before VWAP has clearly rejected price
- **Borrow/liquidity trap:** theoretical setup is good, practical execution is terrible

## Stop Loss

- Standard entries stop at the day high
- VWAP failure entries stop on a reclaim of VWAP

**Sizing rule**

- Use the shared account-risk framework, but keep in mind he treats shorts more selectively than longs
- Rare exceptional shorts have used more risk, but baseline risk discipline still applies

## Profit Targets

- Primary targets are the 10-day SMA and 20-day SMA
- Those moving averages act as natural bounce magnets during the mean reversion
- He describes well-timed versions of this setup as capable of 5-10R outcomes

## No-Trade Conditions

- The stock is only on day 1 or day 2 of the move
- There is no first red day, OR low break, or VWAP failure yet
- The short thesis depends only on “it looks too high”
- Borrow, spread, or liquidity make execution sloppy

## Disciplined Execution Sequence

1. Confirm the move is truly parabolic by the correct threshold for its price bucket.
2. Wait for day 3 or day 4 behavior.
3. Choose one of the three valid entry methods only after failure appears.
4. Set the stop at the day high or VWAP reclaim immediately.
5. Cover into the move toward the 10-day / 20-day moving averages rather than demanding the exact top.

## Important Context

### Qullamaggie's Honest Take On Shorts

> "Guys, don't short. You don't ever have to short. You can make, you can average 100, 200 percent per year just trading the long side."

- He does short, but treats it as a more selective, more timing-sensitive setup than the long side
- He has taken outsized risk only in rare exceptional short situations, not as a baseline rule
