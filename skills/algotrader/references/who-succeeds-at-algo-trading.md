# Who Succeeds at Algo Trading?

## The Institutional Tier: Physics/Math PhDs, Not Finance People

The clearest data point is Renaissance Technologies (Medallion Fund: 66% gross annual returns since 1988 — no one else is even close). Their hiring philosophy is explicit:

> "Wall Street experience is frowned on and a flair for science is prized."

They deliberately do not hire anyone from financial or business backgrounds. Instead: mathematicians, physicists, astronomers, signal processing researchers, computer scientists. Peter Brown and Robert Mercer — who ran it for decades — were IBM speech recognition researchers. Simons himself was a mathematician/code-breaker.

This pattern holds across the elite tier: Two Sigma, D.E. Shaw — all built around PhD scientists from non-finance fields, not Wall Street veterans.

**The insight:** Markets are a signal extraction problem. Physicists and statisticians are trained to find weak signals in noisy data. Finance training actually seems to hurt — it loads you with conventional wisdom and narrative thinking.

---

## The Retail Tier: What Actually Matters

For independent/retail algo traders, raw IQ or credentials matter far less. The research points to:

| Trait | Why It Matters |
|-------|----------------|
| Conscientiousness / discipline | Sticking to rules when your gut says otherwise |
| Risk management obsession | 83% of successful algo traders cite this as #1 (over returns) |
| Scientific method mindset | Hypothesis → test → reject; not narrative-fitting |
| Execution precision | Strategy edge erodes from poor fills |
| Emotional detachment | "Many brilliant people are horrible traders" |

---

## The Retail Structural Advantage

Counterintuitively, retail traders have a real structural edge: they can play in markets too small for institutions. Microcaps, exotic pairs, illiquid instruments — big funds physically cannot enter without moving the market. A retail algo can generate meaningful returns in spaces institutions are forced to ignore.

---

## The Pattern in Summary

- **Institutional success**: PhD physicists, mathematicians, computer scientists with zero finance background. Scientific method applied ruthlessly to noisy data.
- **Retail success**: Discipline and risk management trump intelligence. Emotional control > quant skills. Exploit small/illiquid markets institutions can't touch.
- **What doesn't predict success**: Wall Street experience, finance degrees, conventional trading intuition, raw intelligence alone.
