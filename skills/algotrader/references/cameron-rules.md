## Stock Selection: The 5 Pillars

Every tradable setup must satisfy all five. No exceptions.

**Pillar 1 — Relative Volume: ≥5×**
Stock must be trading ≥5× its 30-day average volume. Confirms extraordinary interest, not routine activity.

**Pillar 2 — Daily % Change: ≥10%**
Stock must be up ≥10% from prior close. Hard filter. > "I'm not interested in stocks that might move, are 'primed' to move, or are 'significantly undervalued.'" He wants stocks already moving.

**Pillar 3 — News Catalyst**
A fundamental catalyst must exist: earnings beat, FDA approval, partnership, government contract. Manually verified — scanners don't auto-filter news. No catalyst = no trade.

**Pillar 4 — Price Range: $1-$20**
Sweet spot: $5-$10. Below $1 avoided (spread risk, OTC issues). Above $20 moves more slowly in percentage terms.

**Pillar 5 — Float: Under 10M shares**
Most critical filter. Under 10M is low float. Under 5M is "rocket fuel." Under 20M is acceptable.

> "Stocks with a float of under 10 million shares will produce some of the biggest percentage gainers." A 5M-share float stock can trade 20M+ shares in a day — extreme supply/demand imbalance.

### Gapper Quality Ratings

| Grade | Float | Short Interest | Catalyst | Pre-Market Volume |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (take it) | <20M | >10% | Strong | >150,000 shares |
| B (selective) | 50-100M | 5-10% | Weak | <100,000 shares |
| C (avoid) | >100M | <5% | Gap <4% | — |

**Gap threshold:** Gaps >4% are tradable. Gaps <4% tend to fill. < "Gaps of more than 4% are good for Gap and Go! trading. Gaps under 4% tend to fill, making them less attractive."

## Scanner Infrastructure

**Pre-market Gap Scanner:** Fires from pre-market open through 9:30am. Scans all stocks gapping up that meet price and volume filters. This builds the morning watchlist.

**High of Day (HOD) Momentum Scanner:** Real-time alerts when a stock makes a new intraday high above prior resistance on above-average momentum. Used for continuation setups throughout the morning. Cameron calls this his "strongest scanner."

**Surging Volume Scanner:** Sorts by highest relative volume in last 5 minutes. Identifies the most active stock right now.

**Pre-market review timing:**
- ~7:00-7:12am ET: Opens gap scanner, reviews top 5-10 gappers
- ~9:00am ET: Final pre-market watchlist review
- Watchlist finalized before 9:30am open

**Focus rule:** Identifies the "most obvious stock on the day" — one stock with the clearest momentum and cleanest setup. Does not spread attention across many names.

## Setup 1: Gap and Go (Primary Opening Setup)

Used 9:30-10:00am ET only. Core setup for the first 30 minutes.

### Criteria

1. Gap >4% in pre-market with fundamental catalyst
2. Mark pre-market highs and any pre-market flag formations
3. All 5 Pillars satisfied

### Entry Triggers at 9:30am (Two Methods)

**Method A — 1-Minute Opening Range Breakout:**
Buy the high of the first 1-minute candle after 9:30am.
Stop: low of that same candle.

**Method B — Pre-Market High Break:**
Buy when price breaks above the pre-market high.
Stop: pre-market low.

### Failed Gap Identification

> "A good gap will hold the top of the gap. A bad gap will be selling off in pre-market."

If the stock is declining in pre-market despite the gap: skip it.

### Time Window

Profits usually realized by 10am ET. Gap and Go trades are specifically bounded to the first 30 minutes.

## Setup 2: Bull Flag (Continuation Setup)

Used throughout morning session. Works on both 1-minute chart (pre-11:30am) and 5-minute chart (all day).

### Pattern Structure

**The pole:** Large green candle(s) representing the initial breakout — the flagpole.

**The flag:** 2-4 candles of sideways-to-slightly-downward consolidation near the top of the pole, with volume noticeably lighter than the pole.

### Valid vs. Invalid Flags

**Valid:**
- Consolidation holds above 50% of the pole's range
- Volume dries up during consolidation (fewer sellers)
- Upper resistance is clear and roughly horizontal (flat top)
- No aggressive selling (no large red candles)

**Invalid:**
- Retracement below 50% of the pole
- Volume spikes during pullback (sellers in control)
- Multiple large red candles suggesting reversal

### Entry (Two Timeframe Methods)

**1-Minute Chart (pre-11:30am):**
Buy the first candle to make a new high after consolidation — the moment the green candle breaks the high of the prior red candle. Cameron sometimes takes a starter position slightly before the break and adds as it confirms.

**5-Minute Chart (after 11:30am):**
Buy the first 5-minute candle to make a new high. > "After 11:30am he prefers to only trade off the 5-minute chart" because the 1-minute chart becomes too choppy in mid-day.

### Stop Loss

Stop at the bottom of the consolidation area (lowest point of the pullback). If the stop is >20 cents away: > "He may decide to stop out minus 20 cents and come back for a second try."

Cameron uses mental stops, not hard stop orders.

### Flat Top Breakout (Variation)

Consolidation forms a flat ceiling of resistance across multiple candles. Entry: break above the flat ceiling. Stop: below the flat base. Identical execution to the bull flag.

## Risk Management

### 2:1 Profit-Loss Ratio (Non-Negotiable Pre-Trade Check)

Every trade must have a minimum 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio before entry.

> "If you do not have the potential to win twice as much as you risk, you should not be taking the trade."

Example: risking $0.20/share → target must be ≥$0.40/share.

### Daily Circuit Breaker

**Max loss per account: $2,000. Total across both accounts: ~$4,000.**

If down $2,000 in a day: stop trading. No exceptions. Called a personal "circuit breaker." He has documented hitting this on specific red days.

### Position Sizing

Fixed-dollar-risk-per-share approach:

```
shares = acceptable_dollar_loss / stop_distance_per_share
```

- Uses fixed share sizes like 3,000 or 6,000 shares as anchors, adjusted by price and stop distance
- Tighter stop → larger share size possible; wider stop → smaller share size
- Scales into positions: take starter (half-size or less) before the trigger, add second half on confirmation

### Scaling Out (Profit Taking)

**Half-out rule:** "Ross sells 1/2 position when he hits his first profit target."
- After selling first half: move stop to break-even on the remainder
- Hold second half with zero additional risk; let it run

### Exit Triggers

- **First red candle rule:** If first half hasn't been sold yet, the first candle to close red signals exit
- **Breakout or Bailout:** > "If the breakout doesn't happen fast, he's out. If it's not going up, he's not holding and hoping."
- **Breakeven stop:** After selling half at target 1, remaining half protected by stop at entry price

### Losing Streak Protocol (Trader Rehab)

1. Set hard share-size cap with broker
2. Reduce daily max loss to 25-50% of normal
3. Extreme cases: one trade per day until confidence rebuilds

## Intraday Timing

### Primary Trading Window: 9:30am-11:30am ET

> "The mornings are almost always the best time to trade, specifically the first hour the market is open."

| Window | Activity |
|---|---|
| 9:30-10:00am | Gap and Go; most trades exited by 10am |
| 9:30-11:00am | Core momentum window; 1-min and 5-min charts |
| 11:00-11:30am | Transition; setup quality degrades |
| After 11:30am | 5-min chart only; fewer, more selective trades |

### Why He Stops Early

Mid-day volume dries up, spreads widen on small-caps, institutional activity drops. The supply/demand imbalance that creates morning momentum dissipates.

> "My average trades are five minutes long. They're very short."

## Honest Caveats

1. **2020 was an anomaly.** Cameron's $4.5M net year coincided with the highest-ever small-cap volatility and volume in history (pandemic retail surge). 2023 shows a more representative current-market baseline (~$306k).

2. **His 2023-2024 results show how much market conditions matter.** The same strategy produced 15× more in 2020 than in 2023 without any change in approach.

3. **The FTC found the vast majority of paying students lost money.** Cameron's results are real and audited. His students' results are not comparable. The FTC prohibited making earnings claims without adequate disclosures.

4. **Scanner platforms have changed.** His Trade Ideas files available publicly are outdated. He now uses Warrior Trading's proprietary Day Trade Dash scanner.

5. **This is explicitly a low-float small-cap morning momentum strategy.** Does not apply to large-caps, ETFs, crypto, or stocks without the specific float/volume/catalyst combination.
