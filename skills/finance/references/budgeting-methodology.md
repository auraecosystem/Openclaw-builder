# Budgeting Methodology

## YNAB's 4 Rules

1. **Give Every Dollar a Job** — Allocate all income to specific categories before spending. Zero-based: income minus all allocations = $0. No "unassigned" money floating around.

2. **Embrace Your True Expenses** — Annualize irregular costs (car insurance, gifts, annual subscriptions) and set aside monthly. $1,200 car insurance = $100/month set-aside. Prevents "surprise" expenses.

3. **Roll With the Punches** — Overspent on dining? Move money from clothing. Budgets are living documents, not rigid constraints. Adjust categories without guilt — the goal is awareness, not perfection.

4. **Age Your Money** — Goal: spend money earned 30+ days ago. Breaks the paycheck-to-paycheck cycle. When you're spending last month's income, you have a full month's buffer.

## 50/30/20 Rule

Split **after-tax** income:
- **50% Needs:** Rent/mortgage, groceries, utilities, transportation, insurance, minimum debt payments
- **30% Wants:** Dining out, entertainment, subscriptions, shopping, hobbies
- **20% Savings/Debt:** Emergency fund, investments, extra debt payments

**HCOL adjustment:** In Toronto/Vancouver, housing alone may exceed 50%. Use 60/20/20 or 65/25/10 and focus on increasing income.

## Mapping Your Simplii Data to Budget Categories

**Needs (from CC categories):**
- Transportation
- Retail and Grocery (groceries portion)
- Health and Education
- Professional and Financial Services (insurance, banking)

**Wants (from CC categories):**
- Restaurants
- Entertainment
- Personal and Household Expenses (discretionary)
- Foreign Currency Transactions (travel)

**Chequing funds_out:** Categorize by description keywords — rent, utilities, phone, internet → Needs. Everything else review manually.

**Chequing funds_in:** Income (salary, e-transfers in).

## Monthly Review Ritual

1. Run analysis script to generate monthly summary
2. Check: Did spending stay within 50/30/20 targets?
3. Identify top 3 spending categories — any surprises?
4. Compare to prior month — lifestyle creep?
5. Calculate savings rate — trending up or down?
6. Adjust next month's targets based on findings
