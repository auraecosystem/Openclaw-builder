# Canadian Couch Potato Investing

## Philosophy

Buy-and-hold globally diversified index ETFs. Minimize fees. Rebalance annually. Ignore market noise. Time in market beats timing the market.

## All-in-One ETF Portfolios

One fund, one purchase, auto-rebalanced. Best solution for most DIY investors.

| Risk Level | Vanguard | iShares | BMO |
|-----------|----------|---------|-----|
| 100% equity | VEQT | XEQT | ZEQT |
| 80% equity / 20% bonds | VGRO | XGRO | ZGRO |
| 60% equity / 40% bonds | VBAL | XBAL | ZBAL |
| 40% equity / 60% bonds | VCNS | XCNS | ZCON |
| 20% equity / 80% bonds | VCIP | XINC | — |

**MERs:** 0.20-0.25% (vs 2%+ for mutual funds). This difference compounds massively over decades.

## Which Risk Level?

- **20+ years to retirement, comfortable with volatility:** VEQT/XEQT (100% equity)
- **10-20 years, moderate risk:** VGRO/XGRO (80/20) or VBAL/XBAL (60/40)
- **<10 years or low risk tolerance:** VCNS/XCNS or VCIP/XINC
- **Rule of thumb:** bonds% roughly equals your age (aggressive) or age minus 10 (moderate)

## Account Location (Tax Efficiency)

- **RRSP:** Hold US/international ETFs here to avoid 15% US withholding tax on dividends
- **TFSA:** Canadian equities or all-in-one ETFs (growth is permanently tax-free)
- **Non-registered:** Canadian dividend stocks (eligible for dividend tax credit), or growth-oriented ETFs (defer capital gains)

With all-in-one ETFs, this distinction matters less — just hold the same ETF across all accounts for simplicity.

## Key Principles

- **Don't pick stocks.** Most active managers underperform index funds after fees.
- **Don't time the market.** Invest regularly regardless of market conditions (dollar-cost averaging).
- **Rebalancing:** All-in-one ETFs handle this automatically. If using multi-ETF portfolio, rebalance annually or with new contributions.
- **Stay the course:** The biggest risk is panic-selling during downturns. A boring portfolio you stick with beats an exciting one you abandon.
- **Fees matter enormously:** 1% extra MER on $100K over 30 years costs ~$90K in lost growth.

## Getting Started

1. Open a self-directed brokerage account (Questrade, Wealthsimple, National Bank Direct)
2. Pick one all-in-one ETF matching your risk tolerance
3. Set up automatic contributions (monthly or per-paycheque)
4. Buy the ETF. Ignore the news. Repeat.
