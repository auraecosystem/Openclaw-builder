# Wealth Building: Starting Late (30s-40s)

## Core Truth

Starting late means being **aggressive with savings rate**, not with investment risk. A 40% savings rate matters more than chasing 2% extra returns.

## Priority Order

1. **Eliminate high-interest debt (>7%)** — Credit cards (20%+), personal loans. Guaranteed "return" equal to the interest rate. Use avalanche method (highest rate first) for math, or snowball (smallest balance first) for motivation.

2. **Emergency fund: 3-6 months expenses** — Park in TFSA high-interest savings account. This prevents debt spirals when surprises hit. 3 months if stable job, 6 months if self-employed or variable income.

3. **Max tax-advantaged accounts** — FHSA (if eligible), then TFSA + RRSP based on your situation. Unused room carries forward — catch-up contributions are your biggest advantage.

4. **Invest the rest** — Non-registered, following Couch Potato strategy.

## Catch-Up Strategies

- **Check your unused room:** Log into CRA My Account → TFSA room + RRSP deduction limit. You may have tens of thousands in unused room.
- **Lifestyle arbitrage:** Keep expenses flat as income grows. Direct every raise entirely to savings/investing.
- **Automate:** Set up automatic transfers on payday. What you don't see, you don't spend.
- **Savings rate ladder:** Increase by 1% of income with every raise until you hit 25-30%.

## The Compounding Math

| Monthly Investment | 7% Return / 25 Years | 7% Return / 15 Years |
|-------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| $500 | ~$405,000 | ~$158,000 |
| $1,000 | ~$810,000 | ~$317,000 |
| $1,500 | ~$1,215,000 | ~$475,000 |

Every year you delay costs roughly 7-10% of your final portfolio. Starting now at any amount beats waiting for a "perfect" time.

## 30s-40s Specific Advice

- **Peak earning years are ahead** — Income typically peaks 45-55. Build the habits now.
- **Don't over-allocate to housing** — Keep total housing costs under 30% of gross income.
- **Insurance matters now** — Term life insurance if you have dependents. Disability insurance if your income supports others.
- **Avoid lifestyle creep** — The single biggest wealth destroyer for mid-career earners.
- **Don't try to "make up for lost time" with speculation** — Crypto, meme stocks, leveraged ETFs. Steady index investing wins.
- **Your human capital is your biggest asset** — Invest in skills that increase earning power.
