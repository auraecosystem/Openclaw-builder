# TFSA, RRSP & FHSA Priority Order

## Investment Priority (Top to Bottom)

1. **Employer RRSP match** — Free money. Always contribute enough to get the full match.
2. **FHSA** (if eligible) — Tax-deductible contributions AND tax-free withdrawals for first home. Best of both worlds. $8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime. Even if not buying soon, open one to start accumulating room; can transfer to RRSP later with no tax hit.
3. **High-interest debt** — Pay off anything above ~5-7% before investing.
4. **TFSA vs RRSP** — See decision matrix below.
5. **Non-registered account** — After maxing tax-advantaged accounts. Prefer Canadian dividend stocks (dividend tax credit) or growth stocks (defer capital gains).

## 2026 Contribution Limits

| Account | Annual Limit | Cumulative (since inception) |
|---------|-------------|------------------------------|
| TFSA | $7,000 | $109,000 (if eligible since 2009) |
| RRSP | $33,810 or 18% of prior year earned income (whichever is lower) | Unused room carries forward |
| FHSA | $8,000 | $40,000 lifetime |

**RRSP deadline:** March 2, 2026 for 2025 tax year deduction.

## TFSA vs RRSP Decision

**Prioritize RRSP when:**
- Marginal tax rate > ~30% (income roughly > $55K in Ontario)
- Expect lower tax rate in retirement than now
- Want to reduce taxable income for benefit clawbacks (CCB, GST credit, OAS)
- Employer offers matching contributions

**Prioritize TFSA when:**
- Lower income years (marginal rate < 30%)
- Need flexibility (TFSA withdrawals are penalty-free, room restored next Jan 1)
- Uncertain future tax bracket
- Already have substantial RRSP and want diversification
- Saving for non-retirement goals

## Key Strategies

- **Spousal RRSP:** Higher earner contributes to spouse's RRSP, gets the deduction. Spouse withdraws in retirement at their lower rate. 3-year attribution rule: withdrawals within 3 years attributed back to contributor.
- **TFSA withdrawal trick:** Withdraw in December, full amount (including growth) re-added as contribution room on January 1, plus new annual room.
- **FHSA → RRSP transfer:** If you don't buy a home, transfer FHSA to RRSP without using RRSP room. Deadline: 15 years after opening or age 71.
- **Unused room carries forward:** Check your RRSP and TFSA room on CRA My Account. Catch-up contributions are powerful for late starters.
