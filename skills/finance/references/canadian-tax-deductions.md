# Canadian Tax Deductions & Receipt Guide

Keep all receipts for **6 years minimum** per CRA requirements. If audited, no receipt = no deduction.

## Employed (T4 Income)

- **Home office:** Flat rate $2/day worked from home (max $500/yr), or detailed method tracking actual rent/utilities/internet as % of workspace area. Detailed method requires signed T2200 from employer.
- **Union & professional dues:** 100% deductible if required for employment. Includes licensing, certifications, mandatory association fees.
- **Tradesperson's tools:** Deduct cost of tools required by employer above $1,368 threshold (2025). Keep itemized list + receipts. Requires T2200.
- **Moving expenses:** Deductible if you moved 40+ km closer to a new job or school. Includes transport, temporary lodging, lease-breaking costs, legal fees for new home.

## Self-Employed / Freelance

- **Home business use:** % of rent/mortgage interest, property tax, utilities, insurance, internet based on workspace square footage. Must be principal place of business or used regularly for client meetings.
- **Vehicle:** Fuel, insurance, maintenance, lease/CCA. **Must keep a detailed km logbook** (business vs personal). No logbook = denied on audit.
- **Office supplies & software:** Paper, ink, postage, business software subscriptions (100%).
- **Meals & entertainment:** 50% of business-related costs. Note attendees + business purpose on receipt.
- **Advertising & marketing:** 100% deductible (website, ads, flyers, sponsorships).
- **Professional development:** Courses, conferences, books directly related to your business.

## Credits (Everyone)

- **Medical expenses (METC):** Claim amounts exceeding lesser of 3% net income or ~$2,833. Eligible: prescriptions, dental, vision, physio, hearing aids, medical devices, medical travel, premiums for private health insurance. Keep every receipt.
- **Charitable donations:** 15% federal credit on first $200, 29% on amounts above $200. Official donation receipts required.
- **Childcare:** Lower-income spouse claims. Covers daycare, before/after school care, day camps, babysitters. Max varies by child age ($8,000-$11,000).
- **Tuition (T2202):** Federal credit of 15% on eligible tuition. Unused amounts carry forward or transfer to spouse/parent.
- **Disability Tax Credit (DTC):** If applicable, unlocks RDSP eligibility and retroactive claims.

## What to Keep (Receipt Checklist)

Medical bills, pharmacy receipts, dental/vision invoices, charitable donation receipts, childcare receipts + provider SIN, T2202 tuition slips, moving receipts, home office utility bills, vehicle logbook, professional dues statements, tool purchase receipts.
