# Ross Cameron Bull Flag / First Pullback

Continuation setup used after the first impulse move. The stock surges, pauses in a tight orderly pullback, then resumes higher.

## Pattern Structure

**The pole**

- Large green candle or sequence of candles representing the first impulse move

**The flag**

- Short sideways or slightly downward consolidation near the highs
- Usually 2-4 candles
- Volume should dry up during the pullback

## Scanning For Candidates

This is usually found from the HOD momentum scanner or a running-up alert after the stock is already in play.

Look for:

- A stock that still passes the 5 Pillars
- Strong morning momentum and a clean first impulse move
- The first orderly pullback, not the fourth sloppy one
- A shallow pause near highs
- Volume contraction during the pullback

Exclude immediately:

- Pullbacks deeper than roughly 50% of the pole
- Large red candles or obvious distribution in the pullback
- Midday 1-minute chop after the best time window
- Stocks that are no longer the leading momentum name

## Valid Pattern Traits

- Pullback holds above 50% of the pole
- No heavy red volume dumping
- Clear resistance point for the breakout
- Stock remains technically strong, not loose and sloppy

## What A Good Setup Looks Like

- First pullback after the initial squeeze
- Two to four tight candles
- Pullback remains above half the pole
- Volume dries up clearly
- Breakout trigger is obvious
- The stock is still the main focus of momentum traders that morning

## What A Weak Setup Looks Like

- Too many candles in the flag
- Retracement is too deep
- Pullback gets loose and noisy
- Volume expands on the selloff
- The stock is still green but no longer acting like the leader

## Trap Setups And Red Herrings

- **Fake bull-flag trap:** trader labels any pause after a spike as a flag even when the structure is sloppy
- **Second-or-third-pullback trap:** later pullbacks are often lower quality than the first one
- **Midday-chop trap:** trying to force a 1-minute bull flag after the morning edge has decayed
- **Heavy-seller trap:** the pattern looks fine on candles but Level 2 shows real seller pressure overhead

## Invalid Pattern Traits

- Pullback retraces too deeply
- Multiple large red candles
- Volume expands on the selloff instead of contracting
- The stock loses the “tight near highs” structure

## Entry

**1-minute chart**

- Buy the first candle to make a new high after the pullback

**5-minute chart**

- After late morning, the same concept applies on the 5-minute chart

This is also the practical “first pullback” trade inside the Ross Cameron framework.

## Time Rules

- 1-minute chart is preferred before roughly 11:30am ET
- After 11:30am, shift to the 5-minute chart because the 1-minute chart becomes choppier

## Stop Loss

- Stop goes under the low of the flag / consolidation
- If the required stop becomes too wide for the setup quality, he may cut the attempt quickly and revisit on a second try

**Practical stop-width note**

- In the source framing, a stop wider than roughly $0.20 can be a warning sign that the setup may be too loose

## Management Rules

- Require at least 2:1 reward to risk before entry
- Often scale out on the first extension
- First red candle can be the exit if the trade has not already partially paid
- If the breakout fails immediately, exit rather than waiting for a larger unwind

## Context

- Best before 11:30am on the 1-minute chart
- After that, the 5-minute chart is generally preferred because the 1-minute chart gets choppier

## No-Trade Conditions

- Pullback retraces more than half the pole
- Volume expands sharply on the pullback
- The setup is loose rather than tight
- The resistance trigger is unclear
